# Mobile Affiliate Attribution — Cross-App Revenue Recovery

> **Portfolio lens:** Attribution architecture, cross-app data integrity, merchant onboarding, reconciliation, and incrementality.

## Executive snapshot

Reframed a mobile affiliate measurement gap as a product-routing, data-integrity, and commercial-governance problem. Deep linking, server-to-server events, standardized parameters, parallel reconciliation, merchant enablement, and a geo holdout repaired both customer journeys and revenue reporting.

## Resume-ready impact

- Introduced modular deep linking and server-to-server tracking that reduced routing failures 90% and improved attribution accuracy 25%.
- Recovered roughly $2M in hidden revenue within $15M of attributed revenue and established shared reporting across Engineering, Finance, Sales, and partners.
- Reduced merchant onboarding from 45 days to nine through a one-page integration pack and standardized controls.

## Interview story

### Situation

Attribution broke when customers moved from mobile web into merchant apps. Revenue disappeared from reports, Finance questioned incrementality, users reached the wrong screen, and merchants faced a 45-day integration.

### Task

Repair the journey and event record, prove net-new value, and create one operating view for internal and partner stakeholders.

### Actions

- Selected a replaceable external SDK to recover value within one quarter rather than wait nine months for an internal build.
- Added server-to-server events, standardized UTMs, and a five-percent reconciliation threshold across internal, merchant, and network logs.
- Ran a four-week geo holdout across 20% of markets.
- Simplified onboarding and repositioned the program from cashback participation to measurable merchant growth.

### Results

- Attributed revenue reached $15M, with roughly $2M recovered from hidden transactions.
- Deep-link failures declined 90%, and data accuracy improved 25%.
- Merchant onboarding fell from 45 days to nine.
- The holdout showed a 0.8-point conversion lift and indicated that roughly 35% of attributed revenue was incremental.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Use a modular vendor solution when time-to-value outweighed a nine-month internal build.
- Keep old and new tracking in parallel with rollback above a five-percent discrepancy.
- Distinguish attributed from incremental revenue.

## Leadership signal

Created one dashboard and escalation model across Engineering, Finance, Sales, merchants, and affiliate partners, resolving disputes through shared evidence rather than functional reports.
