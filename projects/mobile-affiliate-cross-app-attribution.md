# Mobile Affiliate Attribution — Cross-App Revenue Recovery

> **Portfolio lens:** Enterprise BI, attribution architecture, data quality, KPI governance, exception monitoring, reconciliation, and executive reporting.

> **Primary resume experience:** McKinsey & Company - Business Analyst, Marketing Analytics (2014 - 2016).

## Executive snapshot

Re-engineered a fragmented mobile-affiliate measurement flow into a governed BI and attribution system. Deep linking, server-to-server events, standardized parameters, common KPI definitions, exception thresholds, parallel reconciliation, and a geo holdout repaired both customer journeys and leadership reporting.

## Resume-ready impact

- Re-engineered cross-app data collection with modular deep linking and server-to-server tracking, reducing routing failures 90% and improving attribution accuracy 25%.
- Standardized KPI definitions, parameters, reconciliation thresholds, and exception reporting across Engineering, Finance, Sales, and partners, recovering roughly $2M in hidden revenue within $15M of attributed revenue.
- Reduced merchant onboarding from 45 days to nine through a one-page integration pack and converted fragmented reports into a shared, decision-ready operating view.

## Interview story

### Situation

Attribution broke when customers moved from mobile web into merchant apps. Revenue disappeared from reports, Finance questioned incrementality, users reached the wrong screen, and merchants faced a 45-day integration.

### Task

Repair the journey and event record, prove net-new value, and create one governed BI view with consistent KPI definitions for internal and partner stakeholders.

### Actions

- Selected a replaceable external SDK to recover value within one quarter rather than wait nine months for an internal build.
- Added server-to-server events, standardized UTMs, common KPI definitions, and a five-percent reconciliation threshold across internal, merchant, and network logs.
- Ran a four-week geo holdout across 20% of markets.
- Built exception monitoring and a shared executive dashboard so mismatches had owners, thresholds, and a consistent remediation path.
- Simplified onboarding and repositioned the program from cashback participation to measurable merchant growth.

### Results

- Attributed revenue reached $15M, with roughly $2M recovered from hidden transactions.
- Deep-link failures declined 90%, and data accuracy improved 25%.
- Merchant onboarding fell from 45 days to nine.
- The holdout showed a 0.8-point conversion lift and indicated that roughly 35% of attributed revenue was incremental.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Use a modular vendor solution when time-to-value outweighed a nine-month internal build.
- Keep old and new tracking in parallel with rollback above a five-percent discrepancy.
- Distinguish attributed from incremental revenue.
- Standardize KPI definitions before automating executive reporting.

## Leadership signal

Created one KPI framework, dashboard, and escalation model across Engineering, Finance, Sales, merchants, and affiliate partners, replacing fragmented functional reports with shared evidence and named exception owners.
