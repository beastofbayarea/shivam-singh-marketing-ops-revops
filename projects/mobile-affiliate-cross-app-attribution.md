## Evidence-grounded premise

The FTC's advertising guidance requires claims and practices to be truthful and non-deceptive. The World Bank's impact-evaluation handbook explains why credible counterfactuals are needed to estimate causal impact. These sources support affiliate attribution that reconciles evidence across apps and uses incrementality rather than assuming that a tracked touch caused revenue.

## Case approach

- Define event identities, timestamps, consent, deduplication, refund, and ownership rules across systems.
- Reconcile affiliate, app, payment, and finance records before optimizing spend.
- Use holdouts or credible comparison designs to estimate incremental conversions.
- Investigate anomalies, incentive gaming, and disputed credit through auditable evidence.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use reconciliation completeness, duplicate/invalid events, incremental lift, disputed commissions, refund-adjusted value, and anomaly closure. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
