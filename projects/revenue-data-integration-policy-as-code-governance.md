# Revenue Data Integration - Policy-as-Code Governance

> **Portfolio lens:** Marketing operations, revenue data integration, reporting quality, metadata standards, and self-service governance.

## Executive snapshot

Converted a four-day, single-expert integration queue into a governed self-service data platform. A 20-point policy engine encoded regional, identity, security, and exception rules while standard connector and infrastructure patterns handled routine requests and routed ambiguity to expert review.

## Resume-ready impact

- Led integration of 15 Oracle, SAP, EDI, SQL, and mainframe systems through standard connector contracts and policy-as-code controls.
- Moved 80% of provisioning requests from four days to 15 minutes while increasing reporting accuracy from 70% to 98%.
- Captured $850K in new market share and avoided roughly $200K in annual licensing cost through a controlled internal platform.

## Interview story

### Situation

Every connection depended on one senior architect's undocumented judgment. Queue time created most of the delay, while incompatible point-to-point links left customer and revenue data stale and reporting unreliable.

### Task

Make revenue-data integration scalable and governed without bypassing institutional knowledge, weakening controls, or introducing a generic vendor platform that could not handle legacy complexity.

### Actions

- Shadowed the architect and translated decision logic into a testable 20-point policy checklist.
- Defined a pass-to-provision and fail-to-review flow, with every exception feeding a new rule or documented decision.
- Validated build-versus-buy through a three-system MVP that exposed older-system incompatibilities.
- Standardized connector and identity contracts across all 15 systems and created a governance dashboard for data quality, exception ownership, and reporting reliability.

### Results

- Eighty percent of requests became ready in about 15 minutes.
- Reporting accuracy increased to 98%.
- Enterprise clients onboarded ten times faster.
- The platform captured $850K in new market share and avoided $200K in annual recurring license cost.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Accept a three-week schedule dip to create a permanent reusable control plane.
- Make the senior architect the policy author rather than the target of automation.
- Fail closed and preserve expert review for exceptions.

## Leadership signal

Turned stakeholder resistance into durable ownership by treating expert judgment as governed policy and distributing execution while preserving data quality, accountability, and exception control.
