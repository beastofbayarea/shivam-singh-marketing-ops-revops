## Evidence-grounded premise

The NIST Privacy Framework addresses privacy risk in products and services that process personal data. NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 integrates security, privacy, governance, accountability, and supply-chain controls. Together they support revenue-data integration where definitions, permitted uses, access, retention, lineage, and exceptions are executable and auditable.

## Case approach

- Establish canonical customer, account, opportunity, campaign, consent, and revenue definitions.
- Encode access, purpose, retention, quality, and transformation rules in versioned pipelines.
- Preserve lineage from source through model and report, with owned exceptions and approvals.
- Release integrations through parallel reconciliation and reversible cutover.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use definition coverage, policy violations, reconciliation differences, lineage completeness, access exceptions, data freshness, and remediation time. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
